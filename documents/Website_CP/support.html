Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our products and services.

Opening an account is easy. You can apply online through our website, visit any of our branches, or contact our team to schedule an appointment with a financial advisor. You'll need to provide identification documents (HKID or passport), proof of address, and complete our know-your-customer (KYC) form.

The required documents vary by product, but typically include: valid HKID or passport, proof of address dated within the last 3 months (utility bill, bank statement), and source of funds declaration for larger investments. Our advisors will guide you through the specific requirements for your chosen product.

You can access your account through our Client Portal. If you haven't registered yet, click "Login" on our website and select "Register for Online Access". You'll need your policy number and registered email address. Once registered, you can view your portfolio, download statements, and manage your account settings.

Minimum investment amounts vary by product. Our savings plans start from HKD 10,000 per year, while investment products typically require a minimum of HKD 50,000. Premium wealth management services are available for clients with HKD 1 million or more. Contact our advisors for specific product requirements.

Policy changes such as updating beneficiaries, changing contribution amounts, or modifying coverage can be made through your Client Portal, by calling our customer service hotline at +852 2800 1234, or by visiting any of our branches. Some changes may require supporting documentation.

We accept various payment methods including bank transfer, autopay (direct debit), credit card, and cheque. For convenience, we recommend setting up autopay to ensure your premiums are paid on time. Payment methods can be set up or changed through your Client Portal or by contacting our team.

Claims can be submitted online through your Client Portal, by mail, or in person at any branch. You'll need to complete a claim form and provide supporting documents such as medical reports, receipts, or certificates depending on the claim type. Our claims team aims to process claims within 5-10 business days.

Many of our savings plans offer partial withdrawal options after the initial lock-in period, typically 5 years. Early withdrawal may be subject to surrender charges that decrease over time. We recommend discussing your liquidity needs with your advisor before making a withdrawal to understand the impact on your plan.
