About Grand Hudson

Your trusted partner for wealth management in Hong Kong since 2005.

Our Story

For nearly two decades, Grand Hudson has been helping individuals and families in Hong Kong build, protect, and grow their wealth.

Founded in 2005, Grand Hudson began with a simple mission: to make professional wealth management accessible to everyone. What started as a small team of dedicated financial advisors has grown into one of Hong Kong's most trusted wealth management firms.

Today, we serve over 500,000 clients across Hong Kong, Macau, and the Greater Bay Area, managing more than HKD 50 billion in assets. Our success is built on a foundation of trust, expertise, and an unwavering commitment to our clients' financial well-being.
