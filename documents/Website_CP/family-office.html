What is a Family Office?

A Family Office is a private wealth management institution dedicated to serving ultra-high-net-worth individuals and families.

Typically designed for clients with investable assets of USD 50 million to USD 100 million or more, a Family Office provides comprehensive, centralised management of all aspects of a family's financial life.

Services encompass investment management, estate planning, tax optimisation, philanthropic initiatives, and lifestyle services such as property management—all designed to preserve and grow family wealth across multiple generations.
