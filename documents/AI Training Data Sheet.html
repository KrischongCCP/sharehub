Course 1: Generative AI Essentials for End-users

Overview

Generative AI is transforming how professionals across industries create, innovate, and solve problems. This entry-level course immerses participants in the fundamentals of Generative AI (GenAI), societal implications, and familiarizes them with leading AI tools, focusing on responsible and effective usage.

Learning Objectives

  • Comprehend the basics and evolution of GenAI.
  • Understand the societal and ethical impacts of GenAI.
  • Get hands-on with popular AI platforms: ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, DeepSeek, POE, and Google Gemini.
  • Learn the fundamentals of effective prompt creation.
  • Recognize the main risks (AI-generated misinformation, biases) and best practices in data security.

Who Should Attend

  • Professionals interested in leveraging AI in their daily roles.
  • Non-technical staff seeking a foundational understanding of AI.
  • Decision-makers considering adoption of AI tools.
  • HR, operations, marketing, and other business function practitioners.

Course 2: AI Prompt Engineering Essentials for End-users

Overview

With AI tools like ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, POE, and Google Gemini becoming widely accessible, knowing how to get the best output through prompt engineering is essential. This hands-on course delivers practical techniques and structured approaches to writing prompts that drive optimized AI performance.

Learning Objectives

  • Grasp what prompt engineering is and why it matters.
  • Master the anatomy of an effective AI prompt.
  • Develop prompt structures for various use cases (content creation, data analysis, problem-solving).
  • Apply best practices to minimize misunderstandings and maximize AI output relevance.

Who Should Attend

  • Anyone wishing to improve efficiency and output when using AI tools.
  • Staff responsible for content creation, customer service, or data search tasks.
  • Individuals new to AI seeking to build prompt-writing confidence.

Course 3: Advanced AI for Productivity & Workflow Automation

Overview

This comprehensive course bridges the gap between personal productivity and full-scale business automation. Participants will first learn to integrate AI tools into daily workflows for maximum efficiency. Then, they will advance to designing and deploying autonomous AI systems, leveraging Agentic AI, RAG chatbots, and N8n to revolutionize core business processes through the Smartgent platform.

Learning Objectives

  • Identify and integrate AI features in workplace tools to automate routine tasks.
  • Understand the core principles of agentic AI and its role in modern enterprises.
  • Master RAG chatbots to provide accurate, context-aware information from internal knowledge bases.
  • Gain hands-on experience building multi-step business workflows with N8n.
  • Integrate Smartgent, RAG, and N8n to automate a complete, real-world business process.
  • Analyze best practices for security, governance, and scalability of AI-driven automations.

General Audience Guidance

Courses 1-2 are suitable for individuals at a beginner or intermediate level. Course 3 is an advanced course for those who wish to move from personal AI usage to designing and implementing automated business workflows. No coding is required for any course, but a technical aptitude is beneficial for Course 3. Participants will leave with actionable skills and greater confidence in navigating the AI-powered future.
