Docker & CRI-O: Container Lifecycle Management

An intensive training course on mastering containerization from development to production.

Course Overview

Docker is the industry-leading container runtime platform, offering a colossal number of features for container management and orchestration. This training provides a deep dive into the core features of Docker and CRI-O, empowering you with the skills to manage the entire lifecycle of container images effectively. You will gain hands-on experience with image creation, registry services, persistent storage, and complex networking configurations.

Certification & Career Pathways

This training is strategically designed to provide you with the comprehensive knowledge and practical skills required to confidently sit for and pass the industry-recognized **Docker Certified Associate (DCA)** exam. The course curriculum directly aligns with the core competencies of the DCA certification.

Primary Certification Target: Docker Certified Associate (DCA)

A Foundation for Your Career

The skills you learn are a fundamental prerequisite for many other advanced topics and certifications. This course provides the essential foundation for:

Foundation for Kubernetes Certs (CKA, CKAD)

A solid understanding of how to build, manage, and run containers is required before you can tackle Kubernetes.

Cloud Platform Certifications (AWS, Azure, GCP)

Mastering container fundamentals is the first step to working with services like Amazon ECS, Azure Container Instances, or Google Cloud Run.

DevOps Tooling & CI/CD

These principles are crucial for working with CI/CD pipelines and other DevOps tools that rely on containerization.

In short, while the DCA is the sole certification target of this course, the knowledge gained is a critical building block for almost any modern cloud or DevOps career path.

Note: The training provides the knowledge and hands-on experience for exam success. Exam fees are not included in the course fee.
