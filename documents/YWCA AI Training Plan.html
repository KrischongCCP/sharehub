The 12-Month Journey

A phased rollout ensures deep cultural integration before technical scaling.

Month 1

Phase 1: Spark Curiosity

The programme kicks off with **AI Day**. This is about high-visibility excitement. We showcase "impossible" demos—like AI generating a 12-month programme budget in 30 seconds—to break the barrier of skepticism.

Month 3-5

Phase 2: Capability Forge

Intensive training begins. Citizens master prompting, while Champions enter the **AWS AI Hackathon**. By the end of Month 5, YWCA has its first 8 functional AI prototypes built by its own staff.

Month 8-10

Phase 3: Production Pilots

The best prototypes move to live testing. These are "Sandboxed" production environments where AI assists with real beneficiary intake and outreach under strict human oversight.

Month 12

Phase 4: Impact Showcase

Final celebration. We present the data on time saved, lives touched, and service quality improved. The **AI Roadmap for 2027** is formally adopted by the Board.

Safety, Ethics, and
Human Oversight

AI in social service requires a higher standard of care. Our framework ensures we never compromise beneficiary trust for technological efficiency.

Human-in-the-Loop

Every AI output affecting a beneficiary must have a human review step. AI suggests; humans decide.

Sovereign Data Residency

Utilizing Volcano Engine and AWS local zones to ensure data never leaves authorized borders.

Algorithmic Accountability

Regular audits of Champion projects to detect and mitigate bias in social welfare triage.
