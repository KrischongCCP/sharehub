What is SharePoint Data Tiering?

SharePoint data tiering is the process of automatically moving less frequently accessed or inactive data from expensive primary SharePoint storage to more cost-effective archival storage solutions. This helps organizations optimize storage usage and reduce costs by ensuring only active, high-value data remains in primary storage, while older or redundant data is securely archived.

Why is SharePoint Archiving Needed?

Cost Reduction

Unmanaged storage growth leads to higher costs. Archiving stale and ROT (Redundant, Obsolete, Trivial) data minimizes these costs by freeing up expensive storage space.

Compliance & Governance

Many organizations must retain records for legal or regulatory reasons. Archiving supports compliance by enforcing retention policies and ensuring data is kept as required by law.

Future Proofing

Automated archiving manages future storage growth, preventing uncontrolled expansion and associated risks, ensuring your SharePoint environment remains efficient.

What is the ROI?

  • ✔ Direct Cost Savings: By moving inactive data to cheaper storage, organizations avoid paying for additional SharePoint storage.
  • ✔ Operational Efficiency: Automated archiving reduces manual intervention, freeing up IT resources for higher-value tasks.
  • ✔ Risk Mitigation: Ensures compliance with legal data retention requirements, reducing the risk of penalties or legal issues.

Real-World Case Example:

In one real customer scenario, only 7.4% of SharePoint sites and less than 0.2% of files were active, yet storage was projected to grow by over 300 TB annually. Archiving inactive data would significantly reduce this growth and associated costs.

SmarTier – What Is It and How Does It Help?

SmarTier is a SaaS solution designed to automate SharePoint data tiering and archiving. It helps organizations:

  • Automatically identify and archive ROT data based on customizable rules (e.g., file age, size, type, metadata).
  • Move archived content to cost-effective cloud storage (e.g., AWS S3, Azure Blob, Dropbox), while leaving a shortcut (stub) in SharePoint for easy restoration.
  • Enable both end-users and administrators to restore archived files on demand, ensuring business continuity and easy access to historical data.
  • Provide dashboards and reports for monitoring archiving activity, storage savings, and compliance coverage.

User Case and Flow

  1. 1

    Archiving

    When content in SharePoint meets specified criteria (e.g., not modified for over a year, larger than 1MB), SmarTier automatically archives it to a cheaper storage tier. A shortcut with metadata and a restore link is left in SharePoint.

  2. 2

    Restore (End User)

    The user sees the archived file as a shortcut. Clicking it opens a pop-up with information and a restore link. The user confirms, and the file is restored to its original location.

  3. 3

    Restore (Admin)

    Administrators can use the Portal to search for and restore archived content, either in-place or to a new location.

  4. 4

    Monitoring

    Administrators monitor archiving activity, storage savings, and compliance status via a real-time dashboard and exportable reports.

Example Use Case:

A company with rapidly growing SharePoint storage identifies that most files are inactive. Using SmarTier, they set rules to archive documents unmodified for over five years or files larger than 5GB and older than 60 days. This reduces storage costs, ensures compliance with seven-year retention laws, and allows easy restoration if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of storage can SmarTier archive to?

SmarTier supports a variety of cost-effective cloud storage solutions, including AWS S3 (with support for Intelligent-Tiering), Azure Blob Storage, and Dropbox, giving you flexibility in choosing your archival destination.

Is the archived data secure?

Absolutely. Security is a top priority. SmarTier provides end-to-end encryption, ensuring your data is secure both in transit and at rest in the archival storage. Full audit trails are also available for all actions.

Can users get their archived files back easily?

Yes. The process is seamless for end-users. They can restore files on their own through the "stubs" left in SharePoint. These stubs provide a direct link to restore the data, minimizing IT intervention and ensuring users can access historical data when needed.

How is this different from SharePoint's built-in retention policies?

While SharePoint's retention policies are primarily for compliance and preventing data deletion, SmarTier is focused on storage cost optimization. It actively moves data to cheaper storage tiers based on granular rules, while still providing easy access. SmarTier complements native features by tackling the economic challenges of data growth.

Is SmarTier difficult to set up?

No. As a SaaS (Software-as-a-Service) solution, SmarTier is designed for easy integration with your SharePoint Online environment. Our team provides support to ensure a smooth setup and configuration process.
