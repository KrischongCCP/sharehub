Market Commentary

Mid-Year Outlook 2025: Navigating a Shifting Landscape

The first half of 2025 has been characterized by a complex interplay of macroeconomic factors. Inflationary pressures have shown signs of moderating, yet remain above the central banks' target levels. This has led to a cautious stance from monetary policymakers, with interest rate decisions being data-dependent and subject to a high degree of uncertainty.

Equity markets have demonstrated resilience, particularly in the technology and healthcare sectors. However, volatility is expected to persist as geopolitical tensions and supply chain disruptions continue to pose risks. In the fixed-income space, opportunities are emerging in high-quality corporate bonds and government securities, as yields have become more attractive. We maintain a diversified approach, with a focus on risk management and a keen eye on identifying long-term growth opportunities.
